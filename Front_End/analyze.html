How Our System Works

Tafaftire is an AI-powered platform that collects news articles from trusted public news websites through web scraping. The content is then analyzed using Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Machine Learning to predict whether the news is likely to be fake or real.

Trusted News Source Database

We continuously verify content by cross-referencing thousands of trusted international and local news outlets, including:

  • International: BBC News, Reuters, Al Jazeera, CNN
  • National (Somalia): Somali National Television (SNTV), SONNA
  • Local/Regional: VOA Somali, BBC Somali, Hiiraan Online

How Fake News Spreads

Where It Comes From: Fake news often originates from organized networks, bots, or profit-driven sites designed to manipulate public opinion. They use clickbait headlines, manipulated images, and fabricated quotes.

The Stages of Spread:

  • Creation: A fabricated story is published on an untrustworthy website.
  • Seeding: Bots and fake accounts spread the link across social media (Facebook, X, WhatsApp).
  • Amplification: Real people, emotionally triggered, share it rapidly without verifying.
  • Mainstream Pickup: If not caught early, the story often misleads and reaches major media outlets.
