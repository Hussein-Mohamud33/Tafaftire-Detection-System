⚠ Common Fake News Sources

Where Does Misinformation Come From?

Understanding the sources of fake news helps protect yourself. Our AI detects misinformation from all these channels.

SOURCE #1
Fake Official Documents

Forged passports, certificates, and government papers used to spread false narratives and mislead the public.

SOURCE #2
Social Media Misinformation

Viral posts, manipulated images, and fabricated stories spread rapidly across social platforms, reaching millions instantly.

SOURCE #3
TV & Broadcast Media

Misleading broadcasts, manipulated news segments, and biased reporting on television and online media channels.

✦ About Us

About Tafaftire

Tafaftire is an AI-powered platform that collects news articles from trusted public news websites through web scraping, analyzes the content using Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Machine Learning, and predicts whether the news is likely to be fake or real.

Our Mission

To provide a simple, accurate, and real-time verification tool so individuals and media organizations can critically assess news.

Our Vision

A world with an open information environment where truth prevails over manipulated and distorted news.
